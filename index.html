DEUTSCHPASS · iOS & ANDROID

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

DeutschPass ("the app") helps you prepare for German language exams from A1 to C1. It is made by Studio Ekin. This policy explains what information is handled when you use it.

What the app itself stores

Your progress (completed exercises, mock exam scores, vocabulary reviews) and settings (explanation language) are saved only on your device. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in, and the app itself does not send this information anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Microphone and speech recognition

In the speaking section you can answer out loud. The app then uses the microphone and your device's speech recognition service to turn your German into text, so your answer can be checked. The app does not record, keep or upload your voice itself. Depending on your device, the speech recognition service of your operating system (Apple on iOS, Google on Android) may process the audio on its servers under its own privacy policy. Using the microphone is optional: if you do not allow it, you can type your answers instead.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner at the bottom of the screen and occasional full-screen ads between exercises. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; advertising ID on Android)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions and diagnostic information (for example crash and performance data)

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool; you can change your choice at any time with "Privacy options" at the bottom of the home screen. On iPhone the app asks for permission before it may use your advertising identifier, and you can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Children

The app is intended for a general audience of teenagers and adults preparing for German exams and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Not affiliated with exam providers

DeutschPass is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Goethe-Institut, telc gGmbH or any other exam provider. Exam names are used only to describe which exam formats the practice material follows.
